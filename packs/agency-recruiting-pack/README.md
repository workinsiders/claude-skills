# Agency Recruiting Pack: 6 free Claude skills for staffing agencies

The six jobs an agency recruiter does every day, as installable skills. Full page with "where to start" and install steps for every app: [workinsiders.com/skills/agency-recruiting-pack/](https://workinsiders.com/skills/agency-recruiting-pack/)

| Skill | Use when | You get |
|---|---|---|
| [client-bd-outreach](skills/client-bd-outreach/SKILL.md) | You want job orders from companies that are hiring now, without sending spam | A short BD email or four-touch sequence built on one real hiring signal |
| [job-order-intake](skills/job-order-intake/SKILL.md) | A new req came in and you need to know if it is worth working | A one-screen job order with every gap marked and a score out of 12 |
| [job-description-cleanup](skills/job-description-cleanup/SKILL.md) | The client's job description reads like a policy manual | A 250 to 400 word post that leads with outcomes, plus a wording check |
| [boolean-xray-sourcing](skills/boolean-xray-sourcing/SKILL.md) | Your strings return thousands of people or nobody | Strings for LinkedIn, Google X-ray, job boards and your ATS, narrow to wide |
| [candidate-outreach](skills/candidate-outreach/SKILL.md) | You need a first message passive candidates actually answer | A short InMail, email or text plus follow-ups |
| [candidate-submittal](skills/candidate-submittal/SKILL.md) | The client wants evidence, not adjectives | A submittal matched to each must-have, or a blind profile |

**The rule:** the AI drafts the job order, the strings, the messages and the submittal. You choose who to contact, check every fact and press send. No skill ranks or scores a candidate.

## Install

Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add workinsiders/claude-skills
/plugin install agency-recruiting-pack@workinsiders-skills
```

Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok: download the ZIP from the [pack page](https://workinsiders.com/skills/agency-recruiting-pack/).

Fill in `references/agency-profile.md` in each skill first, or use the copy tool on [workinsiders.com/skills/](https://workinsiders.com/skills/) to fill it in once for every skill.
