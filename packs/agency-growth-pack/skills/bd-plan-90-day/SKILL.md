# 90-day BD plan

Agency desks run feast or famine because BD stops whenever searches peak. You help a recruiter set a goal from their own numbers and a weekly habit small enough to keep doing.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read it for specialties, markets, fee basis and guarantee. Never invent fees, averages or conversion rates.

## Input

Ask for whatever the recruiter can give; mark the rest `unknown`:

- Placements and fees over the last 6 to 12 months.
- Where those job orders came from: repeat clients, referrals, past candidates now hiring, cold outreach, inbound.
- Open job orders now and how many are realistically fillable.
- Hours per week they can really give BD.

## Build the plan

1. **The goal.** One number for 90 days, in job orders worked or fees invoiced, built from their own history ("last two quarters averaged 5 job orders a month; target 6"). If there is no history, set an activity goal instead and say why.
2. **Where it comes from.** Split the goal across sources, warm first: existing clients, past clients, referrals, past candidates now hiring, then new accounts. Use their own source mix; do not assume a ratio.
3. **Weekly actions they control.** For example: two existing-client check-ins, three past-client reconnects, one referral ask, one run of outreach. Each with the skill that does it.
4. **Stop doing.** Two or three things that eat BD time with no result in their data.
5. **The weekly BD hour.** A fixed slot on the calendar with a set agenda (below).

## The weekly BD hour

```
1. Replies and follow-ups owed (10 min) -> follow-up-tracker (Recruiter Outreach Pack)
2. Existing clients: who has a new need? (15 min) -> key-client-plan
3. Warm: past clients, referrals, past candidates now hiring (15 min) -> past-client-reconnect, referral-request
4. One new account on a real signal (15 min) -> Recruiter Outreach Pack
5. Book one conversation before the hour ends (5 min)
```

## Output

```
90-DAY BD PLAN | [recruiter] | [start] to [end]
Goal: [number] because [their numbers]
From: existing [n] | past clients [n] | referrals [n] | past candidates [n] | new accounts [n]
Weekly actions: [list, with the skill for each]
Stop doing: [list]
BD hour: [day, time], agenda above
Check-in dates: [day 30, day 60, day 90]
```

## Rules

- Every number is theirs or marked `unknown`. No industry benchmarks.
- Plan only what fits the hours they gave. A plan they cannot keep is worse than a small one.
- Activity goals are fine; promises of revenue are not.
