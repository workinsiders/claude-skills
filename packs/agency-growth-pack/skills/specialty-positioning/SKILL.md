# Specialty positioning

"Full-service staffing for all industries" gives a client nothing to remember. You help a recruiter say who they are for and why, in a line a hiring manager could repeat to a colleague.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read it for specialties, markets, industries and proof points. Never invent placements, retention figures or client names.

## Input

- The agency profile, and the recruiter's last 10 to 20 placements (role, industry, location, how long it took).
- What clients have said about them, in their words, if any.
- Who clients compare them with: other agencies, the client's own TA team, job boards, AI sourcing tools.

## Work through it

1. **Alternatives.** What does the client do if they do not call this desk? List each honestly, including "post the job and wait" and "use an AI sourcing tool".
2. **What the desk has that those do not.** Specific capabilities: a network in one niche, speed on a role type, knowledge of a region, candidates nobody else reaches. Tie each to evidence.
3. **Why it matters to the client.** Outcomes in their terms: fewer bad hires, a seat filled before a line goes down, less time interviewing the wrong people.
4. **Who cares most.** The client situations where this value is highest.
5. **The line.** One sentence: "We fill [roles] for [clients] in [market], [how we're different]."

## Output

```
POSITIONING | [agency or recruiter] | [date]
Alternatives clients have: [list]
What we have that they don't: [capability: evidence]
Why it matters: [outcomes]
Best-fit clients: [situations]
The line: [one sentence]
Short versions: [LinkedIn headline, email signature line, 10-second spoken version]
Proof to show: [placements or facts from the profile]
Weak spots to fix: [claims with no proof yet]
```

## Rules

- Every claim needs proof from the profile or the recruiter's history; mark the rest `needs proof`.
- Never disparage competitors, internal TA teams or tools.
- Plain words. No "best-in-class", "end-to-end", "synergy" or "talent solutions".
- Never use anything under "Words we never use" in the profile.
