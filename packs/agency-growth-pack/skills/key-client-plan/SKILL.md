# Key client plan

The easiest job order to win is the next one from a client who already trusts you. You help a recruiter plan how to deserve it, and how not to depend on one contact.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read it for specialties and terms (exclusivity, guarantee) that affect this client.

## Input

- Placements, open job orders and history with this client.
- The recruiter's notes from calls and meetings, and any public news about the client.
- Who they know there, and how well.

## Build it

1. **What the client is trying to do this year,** in their words from calls or public statements. Mark anything inferred as `to confirm`.
2. **Stakeholder map.** Roles that decide, approve, use and block agency hiring: hiring managers, HR or TA, procurement, the leader who owns the budget. For each person you know: their role, what they have said (quote it), how close you are, last contact.
3. **Thin spots.** Single-threaded relationships, a champion who might leave, procurement you have never met, departments you have never worked with.
4. **Where more work fits.** Teams, sites or role types the desk fills that this client hires, with the evidence (a job post, something they said).
5. **Risks.** Another agency gaining ground, an internal TA team growing, a guarantee replacement that went badly, slow feedback.
6. **90 days of actions.** Dated, each with an owner and the purpose: a quarterly check-in, a market note they would value, an introduction to a second hiring manager.

## Output

```
KEY CLIENT PLAN: [Client] | [date] | owner: [recruiter]
Their goals this year: [ ]
Stakeholders: | Person or role | Decides / approves / uses / blocks | What they said | Closeness | Last contact |
Thin spots: [ ]
More work could come from: [team, evidence]
Risks: [ ]
Next 90 days: | Date | Action | Purpose | Owner |
Review on: [date]
```

## Rules

- Record what people said and did, at work. Never personal details or guesses about private lives.
- Never rate or rank individuals; describe roles and relationships.
- Nothing in the plan promises the client results, timelines or candidates.
