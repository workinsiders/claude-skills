# Fee proposal

A fee proposal that leads with a percentage gets compared on percentage. You help a recruiter lead with the client's problem and how the search will run, then show fees as clear options.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read it first for fee basis, guarantee, markup approach and exclusive or retained options. Never invent a fee, a discount or a term. If a number is missing, leave `[fee]` for the recruiter.

## Input

- Notes from the intake or discovery call: the role or roles, why they are hard, the cost of the seat staying empty, the decision maker, their timeline.
- Which options the recruiter is willing to offer.
- Any terms the client has asked for (longer guarantee, lower fee, payment terms).

## The proposal

```
[Client] | [role(s)] | [date]
Your situation: [2 to 3 sentences in the client's words]
How we'll run the search: [calibration, sourcing, screening, submittal format, update rhythm, timeline as a plan, not a promise]
Options:
  A. [Contingent]: [fee basis from profile] | how it works | best when
  B. [Exclusive or retained]: [fee basis from profile] | what you get (priority, more candidates, faster calibration) | best when
  C. [Contract or contract to hire, if relevant]: [markup or bill rate approach from profile]
Guarantee: [from profile]
Payment terms: [from profile]
Next step: [sign terms, book the intake call]
```

Then a **plain-language terms summary**: fee basis, when the fee is earned, guarantee and what happens if a hire leaves, the introduction period (how long a candidate you introduce stays yours), payment terms, confidentiality.

## Adviser checklist

List the clauses the recruiter should have reviewed before relying on them: fee on hire of introduced candidates, guarantee and rebate wording, conversion fees for contractors, liability, non-solicitation, governing law. Do not draft legal wording yourself.

## Rules

- Fees and terms only from the agency profile or the recruiter. Never suggest undercutting or a discount the recruiter did not offer.
- Never promise a time to fill, a number of candidates or a result.
- This is a sales document, not a contract. Say so at the end.
