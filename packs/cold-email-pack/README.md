# Cold Email Pack: 17 free Claude skills for recruitment agency cold email

Sell your recruitment services by cold email without burning your domain, plus two skills for candidate campaigns. Full page with the copy tool and install steps for every app: [workinsiders.com/skills/cold-email-pack/](https://workinsiders.com/skills/cold-email-pack/)

Start with [cold-email-start-here](skills/cold-email-start-here/SKILL.md). It runs one campaign in order and hands each stage to the right skill.

| Stage | Skill | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | [cold-email-start-here](skills/cold-email-start-here/SKILL.md) | You want to start cold email for your agency and don't know the order |
|  | [recruitment-icp-builder](skills/recruitment-icp-builder/SKILL.md) | You email anyone who might hire and nobody answers |
|  | [recruitment-offer-builder](skills/recruitment-offer-builder/SKILL.md) | Your BD emails say what every other agency says |
| Set up sending | [sending-setup-plan](skills/sending-setup-plan/SKILL.md) | You are about to cold email from your main domain |
|  | [deliverability-check](skills/deliverability-check/SKILL.md) | Replies dropped to zero or your emails land in spam |
| Build the list | [lead-list-builder](skills/lead-list-builder/SKILL.md) | Your list is a data export with no reason to email anyone on it |
|  | [list-verification-gate](skills/list-verification-gate/SKILL.md) | You are not sure the list is safe to send |
| Write the emails | [bd-cold-email-sequence](skills/bd-cold-email-sequence/SKILL.md) | You need a sequence that wins job orders, not one more agency pitch |
|  | [mpc-email-campaign](skills/mpc-email-campaign/SKILL.md) | You have a strong available candidate and want companies to see them |
|  | [email-variants-spintax](skills/email-variants-spintax/SKILL.md) | Hundreds of identical emails are going out from your mailboxes |
| Candidate campaigns | [candidate-cold-email-sequence](skills/candidate-cold-email-sequence/SKILL.md) | Your candidate emails say "exciting opportunity" and nobody replies |
|  | [candidate-database-campaign](skills/candidate-database-campaign/SKILL.md) | A new job came in and your database has people you haven't spoken to in years |
| Check and launch | [cold-email-compliance-check](skills/cold-email-compliance-check/SKILL.md) | You want to know what the law needs before you cold email clients or candidates |
|  | [campaign-launch-checklist](skills/campaign-launch-checklist/SKILL.md) | You are about to press launch |
| Replies and results | [cold-email-reply-handler](skills/cold-email-reply-handler/SKILL.md) | Replies are coming in and each one needs a different answer |
|  | [ab-test-plan](skills/ab-test-plan/SKILL.md) | You keep calling winners from 40 sends |
|  | [campaign-results-review](skills/campaign-results-review/SKILL.md) | The campaign sends, but job orders don't come |

**The rule:** The AI plans the campaign and drafts every email. You choose who to email, approve every word and launch it yourself. Nothing is sent or launched for you, nothing is scraped, candidates are marketed only with their written permission, and legal points go to your adviser.

Two skills hand off to other packs: one-off emails go to [client-bd-outreach](../agency-recruiting-pack/skills/client-bd-outreach/SKILL.md) and [candidate-outreach](../agency-recruiting-pack/skills/candidate-outreach/SKILL.md); fee pushback goes to [fee-objection-replies](../agency-growth-pack/skills/fee-objection-replies/SKILL.md).

## Install


Fill in `references/agency-profile.md` in each skill first, especially the new Cold email section, or use the copy tool on the pack page to fill it in once for every skill.
