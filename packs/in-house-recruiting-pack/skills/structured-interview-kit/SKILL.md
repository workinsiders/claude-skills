# Structured interview kit

You build the kit that makes every candidate meet the same questions, in the same order, judged against the same criteria.

If `references/company-profile.md` exists, read it for your interview stages and accommodation contact.

## Inputs

- The hiring scorecard with four to six criteria. If there is none, ask for the criteria or suggest the hiring-scorecard skill first.
- The interviewers and how long each slot is.

## Build it

1. **Split the criteria.** Give each interviewer one to three criteria. No criterion is covered by nobody; avoid two interviewers covering the same one unless the team wants a second view.
2. **Questions per criterion.**
   - Two behavioural questions about something the candidate has actually done ("Tell me about the last time you...").
   - One situational question drawn from the real job ("In your first month here, X happens. What do you do?").
3. **Probes.** For each question, three follow-ups that pull out specifics: what exactly did you do, what happened next, how did you know it worked. Add one probe that turns an "I would" or "we did" answer back to the candidate's own past actions.
4. **What good sounds like.** Copy the weak and strong evidence lines from the scorecard under each criterion so interviewers listen for the same things.
5. **Running order.** Two-minute opening (who you are, how the interview works, notes will be taken), questions with timings, five minutes for the candidate's questions, closing script (what happens next and when).
6. **Note-taking.** Write what the candidate said, as close to their words as possible. Judge it after the interview, on the evidence form, before talking to anyone.

## Never ask

Include this list in every kit: age or graduation year, family plans or childcare, pregnancy, health or disability (ask only whether they can do the job's essential tasks, with or without adjustments), religion, nationality or accent, marital status, arrests, salary history where restricted, union membership. Flag that rules vary by location and point to the person named for legal questions.


## Rules

- Questions must be job-related and the same for every candidate at that stage.
- Do not score, rank or summarise a candidate from this skill. Evidence goes on the interview-evidence-form.
