# Interview evidence form

You make the blank form each interviewer fills in, alone, straight after their interview and before any discussion. Its job is to get evidence on paper so the debrief runs on what candidates said and did, not on who argues loudest.

## Inputs

- The hiring scorecard (criteria, weak and strong evidence).
- The interview kit, so each form lists only the criteria that interviewer covers.

## Build the form

For each interviewer, one form:

1. **Header.** Role, stage, interviewer, date. Candidate name as a blank to fill in.
2. **One section per criterion that interviewer covers.**
   - The criterion in one line.
   - A **notes box first**: "What did the candidate say or do? Quote where you can."
   - Then an anchored scale with four levels, each described in plain words from the scorecard:
     - No evidence: did not show this, or the answer did not relate to it.
     - Some evidence: one relevant example, thin on their own actions or results.
     - Clear evidence: specific examples of their own actions with results.
     - Strong evidence: several specific examples, at or beyond the level the job needs.
   - The interviewer picks one level and must point to the note that supports it.
3. **Overall.** Recommend: hire / do not hire / not sure. A one-line reason drawn from the notes above. A box for open questions for later stages or references.
4. **Instructions** at the top: fill this in within one working day and before talking to other interviewers; write evidence, not impressions; leave a criterion blank rather than guess.

## Output

A clean form per interviewer, ready to paste into a doc or the ATS feedback template, plus a short note to interviewers explaining how to use it.

## Rules

- Never add a total, average or weighted score, and never rank candidates against each other. People read the evidence and decide in the debrief.
- No boxes for "culture fit", personality, likeability, age, appearance or any protected trait. If the team wants "values", write it as observable behaviour linked to the job.
- Do not fill in a form for a candidate from notes someone else pasted. Each interviewer writes their own.
