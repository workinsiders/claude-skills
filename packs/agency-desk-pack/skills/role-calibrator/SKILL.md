# Role calibrator

What a hiring manager says at intake and what they say yes to are often different. Calibration finds the difference in days, not after a month of rejected submittals.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read the blind profile setting. Calibration profiles are blind (no name, no current employer) and they are not submittals: you have not spoken to these people yet.

## Mode 1: build the calibration set

Input: the job order and 10 to 30 profiles from a first search, as pasted text or summaries.

Pick three to six profiles that differ on purpose, so each reaction teaches you something:

- **The on-paper match:** ticks every box in the job order.
- **The stretch:** strong on the work, missing one listed requirement.
- **The different background:** same skills, from another industry or type of company.
- **The level test:** one level above or below what the job order says.
- **The trade-off:** strong on the top must-have, weak on a nice-to-have the manager mentioned more than once.

Say in one line what each profile tests.

Write each one as a five-line blind summary: current level and type of company, years doing the work, two pieces of evidence, location band, one clear gap. No names, no employer names, no photos, and no schools unless the manager listed education as a must-have.

Then draft the note to the hiring manager:

- One sentence on why: "Before we contact anyone, I'd like your reaction to a few real profiles so we search exactly right."
- For each profile, three questions: Would you interview this person? What do you like? What's missing?
- Ask for replies within two working days, or offer a 15-minute call.

Keep the note under 150 words, plus the profiles.

## Mode 2: read the reactions

Input: the manager's replies or call notes, plus the job order.

Produce:

1. **What changed:** requirements that turned out to matter more, less or not at all. Quote the manager.
2. **The hidden must-have:** what they rejected profiles for that is not in the job order. If there is none yet, say so.
3. **Updated job order:** must-haves (three at most), nice-to-haves and deal-breakers, rewritten.
4. **Search changes:** backgrounds and target companies to add or drop, titles to include, keywords to change. List what to hand to the Boolean and X-ray Sourcing skill.
5. **Red flags:** contradictions ("wants ten years, but rejected everyone above the range"), moving goalposts, or a pay range that does not fit the profiles they liked. Suggest how the recruiter raises each one with the client.

## Rules

- Never contact or submit anyone in the calibration set without speaking to them first and getting permission.
- Remove anything that could reveal age or other protected traits: graduation years, photos, personal details.
- If the manager rejects profiles for reasons tied to protected traits, flag it to the recruiter as a compliance concern. Never add it to the job order.
- Keep the manager's own words. Do not soften what they said.
