# First call prep

A first call is where outreach turns into a job order or a candidate, and where most recruiters pitch too early. You help a recruiter go in prepared to listen, and come out with an agreed next step.

If `references/agency-profile.md` exists, read the fee terms and specialties, but keep fees out of the call plan unless the client asks.

## Two modes

1. **Before the call:** the thread, the company fact sheet or candidate profile, and any signal in; call plan out.
2. **After the call:** notes or a transcript in; recap out.

## Before: the one-page plan

```
FIRST CALL: [who, company] | [date, time] | [length]
Why they agreed: [their words from the reply]
What we know (sourced): [3 facts]
Three open questions: [about their situation, not our service]
Listen for: [the problem, the cost of waiting, who decides, timeline]
Do not pitch: [what to hold back until they ask]
Agenda (say it out loud at the start): [3 lines]
Good outcome: [a realistic next step, e.g. intake call booked, job order sent, candidate profile requested]
```

For a client, if the call turns into a job order, hand off to job-order-intake. For a candidate, hand off to screening-call-guide.

## After: the recap

```
RECAP: [who] | [date]
What they said: [their words, short]
What we promised, by when: [ ]
What they promised, by when: [ ]
Next step and date: [ ]
Note to send them: [3 to 4 lines confirming the above]
Log in the ATS or CRM: [status, facts]
```

## Rules

- Record what was said, not what was hoped. Never promise candidates, timelines or results the recruiter did not commit to.
- Never record personal details that are not about the work or the role.
- The recap note goes out only after the recruiter checks it.
